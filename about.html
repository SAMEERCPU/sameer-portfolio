Aerospace Engineering | UAVs | Intelligent Systems

I’m an aerospace engineering graduate from Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Nepal, originally from Kailali, Sudurpaschim Province. Growing up in a part of Nepal where technology can play an important role in addressing real-world challenges has shaped the way I think about engineering and the kind of work I want to pursue.

My final-year research, “UAV-Based Crop Health Monitoring Using RGB Aerial Imaging,” involved flying a DJI Phantom 4 over maize plots, extracting vegetation indices from orthomosaic imagery, and training a Random Forest classifier to identify potential nutrient deficiencies. I’ve also built a Paper Ranking and Recommendation System Research through Samsung Innovation Campus, designed Aerombulance, a short takeoff and landing aircraft concept for medical transport in remote areas, and developed a multi-agent simulation of adaptive drone swarm behaviour. These projects have allowed me to explore different sides of engineering while bringing together my interests in aerial data, computer vision, programming, and aircraft design.

Beyond research, I enjoy building and experimenting with UAVs, from sourcing components and configuring Pixhawk flight controllers to integrating companion computers for real-time object tracking. I also enjoy sharing what I learn through hands-on drone and RC aircraft workshops for younger students. I like taking an idea from the drawing board to a working system, testing it in practice, and learning from the results.

Coming from Kailali, I’m interested in how engineering and emerging technologies can address real-world challenges across Nepal. I’m continuing to develop my skills in UAV operations, aerial data collection and processing, AI, machine learning, and coding, while exploring where these interests can take me next. For me, the most rewarding part of engineering is turning curiosity into something tangible whether that means building a drone, writing a piece of code, or finding something useful in the data it collects.

Research Interests

My research interests focus on the use of drones and artificial intelligence to address challenges in agriculture. I am particularly interested in building and customizing UAVs for agricultural applications, collecting aerial images and field data, and using computer vision and machine learning to monitor crop health and support precision farming. I am also interested in UAV system integration, flight operations, image processing, and exploring how different imaging technologies can improve agricultural monitoring. Through my research, I hope to combine practical drone development, field data acquisition, and AI-based analysis to develop effective and affordable solutions for farmers.
